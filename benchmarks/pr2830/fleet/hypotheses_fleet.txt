PRE-DECLARED GRID AND HYPOTHESES - multi-map validation of the B9 exploration fix
================================================================================
Written 2026-08-29 before any arm comparison was launched. Nothing below is
widened or tuned afterwards.

ONE THING RAN BEFORE THIS FILE AND IS NAMED HERE SO IT IS NOT HIDDEN: a timing
pilot, stock arm only, config `shipped` only, 4 m lidar, 6 starts on each of the
four new maps, with a 420 s wall cap. It measures how expensive a run is on each
new floor so the grid below can be sized inside the compute budget. It contains
no comparison between arms and no B9 arm, so it can neither confirm nor deny
anything in this file.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE QUESTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The B9 fix (finish-the-branch, geodesic radius R = 9 m, harness-side wrapper in
fix_hysteresis.py) beat stock and PR #2830 on the bigoffice pair at 4 m and did
not hurt at 12 m. Caveat 5 of rapport_fix.md: R = 9 m was one of two declared
values, won by 2 swings on a 12-run sweep, and there is no evidence it
generalises. Caveat 3: bigoffice is one room plus one corridor, not the
multi-hallway floor the dimOS maintainer described. This fleet answers both on
other floors, or says plainly that it cannot.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WORTHINESS GATE (per map, before any bench)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
gate_fleet.py, at 4 m, on the body-passable floor of the 0.46 m body:

  a. DEGENERATE, the rule of gate_midstart.py, unchanged: a start whose first
     lidar revolution already reveals >= 50 % of everything that start can ever
     see is dropped. A map whose every start is degenerate is rejected.
  b. WORTHINESS: the median over the 6 starts of the share of the ceiling that
     appears only after a walk longer than one lidar revolution must be
     >= 20 %. The number is reported for every map, passed or not. 20 % is set
     against the two references already in this workspace: the shipped 12 m
     bench scored 13.8 % on bigoffice and called it thin, and scored 0.0 to
     2.4 % on the four flat maps it then refused to draw conclusions from.
  c. A map with no body-passable floor at all is rejected.

Rejects are listed with their numbers, not silently dropped.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STRUCTURE MEASURE AND CLASS (reported for every map, pass or reject)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - ways out of each of the 6 chosen starts: midstarts.open_runs, 32 rays, a
    direction open when the 0.46 m body can drive 1.5 m along it, contiguous
    angular runs counted. Dead end 1, corridor 2, T 3, crossing 4. A start with
    >= 3 is a junction start. NOTE, because it changes how the number reads: a
    cell in fully open space, every ray open, also returns 1.
  - branches: connected pieces of the body-passable floor left once a geodesic
    ball of radius R around the graph centre is cut out, keeping a piece only if
    it is >= 2 m2 and reaches >= 3 m further out. Swept over R in {3, 5, 8, 12}
    m and reported in full; the class rule uses the maximum over the sweep. The
    sweep is used rather than one radius because the fleet's floors run from 8 m
    to 70 m across and one ball cannot be "the middle" of both.
  - floor width: 2 x the clearance of each passable cell, median, p90, and the
    share above 3 m.

CLASS:
    multi-branch    >= 3 junction starts AND max branches over the sweep >= 3
    single-room     max branches over the sweep <= 1 AND <= 1 junction start
    room+corridor   otherwise

At least one multi-branch map is the prize. If none of the accessible datasets
yields one, the report says so plainly; that limitation matters more than any
count in the tables.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE GRID
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAPS      every map that passed the gate, PLUS bigoffice and bigoffice_hc as
          anchors. The anchors' 4 m four-arm numbers are re-used from
          ../sim_2830_fix/ (results_trace_4m.json and results_fix_4m.json); the
          box was certified bit-identical to the local rig on 48 bigoffice runs
          before this job, and re-running them would buy nothing.
STARTS    6 per map by the midstarts.py rule: centre + mid1..mid5.
ARMS      stock, stock+B9, pr2830, pr2830+B9.
          ON THE NEW MAPS ONLY, the radius question: stock+B6 and stock+B12,
          giving R in {6, 9, 12}.
LIDAR     4 m everywhere. Plus 12 m on the new maps for the regression check,
          arms stock and stock+B9, and pr2830 / pr2830+B9 if the budget allows.
METRICS   everything the fix bench reports, with each map's own swing threshold
          = half the bounding-box diagonal of its body-passable floor.
          BOTH swing counts are reported:
            raw          consecutive issued goals further apart than the
                         threshold, the number the mid-start report quoted;
            real         the same, minus class-N events: the swings where the
                         goal sequence jumped and the ROBOT did not, because the
                         previous goal timed out or was refused and the robot
                         was never on it. The filter is diagnose_swings.py's
                         rule: a swing is class N when the newly chosen goal
                         lies within R_NEAR_M = 6.0 m of the robot at the moment
                         it was issued. diagnose_swings.py measures that 6 m as
                         a geodesic route length, which needs the per-decision
                         trace; the fleet runs without the trace and measures it
                         straight-line, from the robot pose the bench already
                         records with every goal (`from_x`, `from_y`). The two
                         are compared on the 48 traced bigoffice runs, where
                         both exist, and the disagreement is reported before any
                         fleet number is quoted.
          PRIMARY metric = real crossings and real round trips. The raw count is
          reported alongside for continuity with the mid-start report.
          A round trip = a swing followed within the next three jumps by another
          swing whose direction opposes it, counted on the same series as the
          swing count it belongs to.
EXECUTION --jobs <= 190, --maps always explicit, a wall cap on every bench
          invocation and a per-run cap inside the harness. A capped run is
          reported with end_reason wall_cap and counted, never dropped.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HYPOTHESES, verbatim
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H1: on every gated map at 4 m, stock+B9 has fewer real crossings and fewer real
round trips than stock, with median total path not worse than +5 % relative and
median final coverage not worse than -5 % relative.

H2: same statement for pr2830+B9 vs pr2830.

H3 (radius): R = 9 is within 2 real crossings of the best R on every new map
(i.e. no map demands a very different radius).

H4 (regression): at 12 m on the new maps, stock+B9 is not worse than stock
beyond the same budgets.

Each hypothesis is judged per map and overall; a failure on any map is reported
as a failure on that map, not averaged away.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT IS NOT ALLOWED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No radius outside {6, 9, 12}. No margin k sweep. Candidate P stays unrun.
- No map added to the grid after its numbers are seen.
- No threshold in section 1, 2 or 4 changed after the fact.
- Headline counts re-derived from the raw goal coordinates, independently of the
  summarise() fields that wrote them, and the disagreement stated.
