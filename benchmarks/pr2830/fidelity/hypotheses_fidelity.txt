HYPOTHESES - sim_2830_fidelity
Written 2026-08-30, BEFORE any comparative run of this job.
Workspace: .../scratchpad/sim_2830_fidelity/

WHY THIS JOB EXISTS
===================
An external audit of the dimOS exploration dossier found the harness'
temporal semantics unfaithful, on three counts. All three were re-verified
against the installed package in this job before any code was written, and the
verification with its line references is loop_semantics.md. In short:

  (1) TEMPORALITY. dimOS publishes a goal, waits goal_timeout = 15 s
      (wavefront_frontier_goal_selector.py:823), and on timeout re-runs the
      selection WITHOUT CANCELLING ANYTHING (:828 logs a warning and loops).
      The navigator keeps driving the robot toward the OLD goal for the whole
      of the next selection compute, which is their pure-python full-grid BFS
      and costs seconds. Our harness froze the robot for that interval.
  (2) SPEED. The autonomous stack walks at LocalPlanner._speed = 0.55 m/s
      (local_planner.py:67), not the 0.60 m/s the go2 bench derived from
      teleoperated recordings.
  (3) VOCABULARY. Class C is "locally forced under R = 6 m", never
      "legitimate". Class N events are goal churn without displacement and are
      PUBLISHED SEPARATELY, never silently dropped from a table.

WHAT IS BEING RE-JUDGED
=======================
G1 of ../sim_2830_go2/rapport_go2.md: "with the Go2 profile at 15 s shipped,
the cross-map swing behavior the maintainer described is present under stock".
It was declared to hold on >= 2 of the 4 discriminating maps and was reported
as holding on 3 of 4 at 4 m and 4 of 4 at 12 m. Every one of those numbers was
produced with a frozen robot at 0.60 m/s. This job asks whether G1 survives the
corrected instrument, and if it does, whether the remedy still helps.

THE PARAMETER
=============
T_sel = the selection compute, in SIMULATED seconds, per decision.

  T_sel = 0   the OLD arm, in full: robot frozen through the selection AND
              through the 2 s retry wait. This is every earlier bench in this
              workspace, bit for bit. It is kept as an arm so the fidelity
              change is read as a difference, not swapped in silently.
  T_sel > 0   the faithful loop. On a goal whose pursuit ended on the 15 s
              timeout the navigator was never cancelled, so the robot keeps
              walking to that goal for T_sel more seconds; and the goal it is
              handed next was chosen for the pose it had when the compute
              STARTED (selector line 794 reads odometry at the top of the
              loop). On a goal that was REACHED, or that the planner REFUSED,
              the robot really is standing still, so T_sel there is standstill
              and costs simulated time only. That asymmetry is modelled.

SWEEP: T_sel in {0, 5, 15} s. THIS SET IS FIXED BY THIS DECLARATION AND CANNOT
BE CHANGED BY THE MEASUREMENT BELOW.

  An isolated measurement of the real selection cost (one worker, no
  contention, this rig, config shipped, stock, 4 m, four maps, six starts) is
  running while this file is written and will be reported in
  t_sel_measurement.txt whatever it says. It is an ANCHOR for reading the
  sweep, not a gate on it: 5 and 15 bracket the order of magnitude that any
  full-grid python BFS over these grids can have, and the value that matters to
  the verdict is whether the behaviour is stable ACROSS the bracket.
  Cross-check already in hand, from the recorded go2 bench's own decide_ms_mean
  field (384-core box, 24-60 concurrent workers, so an UPPER bound): per-map
  medians 10.2 to 16.8 s at 4 m and 10.5 to 16.1 s at 12 m on the four maps,
  34.6 s on hk_allaround.

RULE FOR STAGE B's T_sel (declared now, so it cannot be chosen after seeing a
result): the swept faithful value CLOSEST to the measured isolated median;
on a tie, the SMALLER one.

THE PROFILE
===========
go2ctrl: the go2 profile of ../sim_2830_go2/go2_profile.py with ONE change,
speed 0.60 -> 0.55 m/s, for the reason in loop_semantics.md section 4. Body
0.31 x 0.70 m, turn 0.63 rad/s, lethal clearance 0.225 m, pivot clearance
0.383 m, scan every 0.25 m: all unchanged. 15 s x 0.55 = 8.25 m of walk before
the loop pulls the robot off a goal, against 9.00 m in the go2 bench.

Numbers in this job are therefore NOT subtractable from the go2 bench's: two
things changed, the speed and the loop semantics. The T_sel = 0 arm here is the
go2 bench's semantics at the corrected speed, and it is what the faithful arms
are compared against.

WHAT RUNS
=========
Maps: the HK floors and nothing else.
  DISCRIMINATING (the grid, the verdicts turn on these four):
    hk_office, hk_park, hk_elevator, hk_entrance
  SET ASIDE, same rule and same reason as the go2 bench: hk_allaround. Its

    goals across, and its scoring configuration has never finished in any job
    in this workspace. If wall clock permits it is run shipped-only and

Starts: the 6 middle starts of midstarts.choose_mid_starts (centre, mid1..mid5),

Ranges: 4 m and 12 m, both, judged separately and never pooled.
Wall cap: 900 s per run, applied identically to every arm; a capped run is
  reported as capped and never quietly dropped.

STAGE A - stock only, does G1 survive?
  arms:    stock
  config shipped:  T_sel in {0, 5, 15}
  config scoring:  T_sel in {0, 15} only.
    PRE-DECLARED EXPECTATION, to be tested rather than assumed: in `scoring`
    the goal timeout is not applied, so no goal ever ends on a timeout, so no
    goal is ever still live during a selection, so T_sel is pure standstill and
    the two runs should be IDENTICAL in space (same path, same goals, same
    coverage) and differ only in simulated time and, through it, in the
    failed-goal filter's 60 s clock. If they are not identical, that is a
    finding about the harness and it is reported as one.
  grid:    4 maps x 6 starts x (3 shipped + 2 scoring) x 2 ranges = 240 runs

STAGE B - only if Stage A shows real crossings at the faithful settings.
  arms:    stock vs stock+M4.3, at the Stage-B T_sel chosen by the rule above
  configs: shipped and scoring, both ranges, same 6 starts, paired
  grid:    4 maps x 6 starts x 2 arms x 2 configs x 2 ranges = 192 runs
  If Stage A's faithful arms show zero real crossings on 3 or more of the 4
  maps at both ranges, Stage B DOES NOT RUN and the report says so: there would
  be nothing for a remedy to remove.

METRICS - the same as every bench in this workspace, re-derived, never re-defined
================================================================================
Everything is recomputed from the RAW published goal coordinates of each run by
a second independent pass (analyse_cand2.load), and its disagreement with the
field the bench wrote while running is printed. The zero-disagreement statement
is a deliverable.

  swing threshold, per map:  half the bounding-box diagonal of the BODY-PASSABLE
      floor of that map (midstarts.body_bbox_diagonal_m / 2). Per map, never
      pooled, never averaged.
  raw swing:      consecutive published goals further apart than that threshold.
  class N:        a raw swing whose new goal is within R_NEAR = 6.0 m of the
      robot AT THE MOMENT THE GOAL TOOK EFFECT. Vocabulary, fixed here:
      class N is GOAL CHURN WITHOUT DISPLACEMENT - the goal sequence jumped
      across the floor and the robot did not have to move to serve it.
      N EVENTS ARE PUBLISHED AS THEIR OWN COLUMN IN EVERY TABLE. They are never
      "dropped", and the word "dropped" is not used about them.
  REAL crossing:  raw swing minus class N. The primary metric.
  round trip:     a real crossing followed within 3 jumps by one whose direction
      opposes it.
  budgets:        median path (m) and median coverage (percentage POINTS of the
      body-visible ceiling from that start), per map, per config.

  A NOTE ON "from where". With a non-zero T_sel the pose the selector saw and
  the pose the robot had when the goal took effect are different. The class-N
  test uses the pose when the goal TOOK EFFECT, because the question class N
  asks is whether the robot had to travel to serve the goal, and it starts
  serving it at the publish. Both poses are recorded per goal (from_x/from_y
  and dec_x/dec_y), they are equal at T_sel = 0, and their difference is
  reported per run as sel_drift_median_m.

  CLASS C VOCABULARY, fixed here and used in every sentence of the report: a
  crossing that survives the N filter and has no available candidate within
  R = 6 m is "LOCALLY FORCED UNDER R = 6 m". It is NOT called legitimate,
  correct, or justified. R = 6 m is a choice of radius, not a fact about the
  floor, and the phrase carries the radius with it.

READING RULES
=============
G1 (restated for this job), per range, per T_sel:
    "under stock, config shipped, real crossings > 0 on at least 2 of the 4
     discriminating maps."
  Reported per map and per T_sel. If G1 fails at T_sel = 5 and 15 while holding
  at T_sel = 0, THAT IS THE HEADLINE OF THIS JOB and everything else is moot;
  it goes first in the report, before any other number.

Stage B, per map, both configs must hold:
    stock+M4.3 has strictly FEWER real crossings than stock, and no more round
    trips, with median path within +5 % and median coverage within -5 points.
  A map where neither arm produces a crossing is NO EVENTS, not a pass.
  Per-map failures are printed with the reason (metric, path budget, coverage
  budget) and no map is averaged away.

WHAT WOULD MAKE ME WRONG
========================
  - If T_sel = 15 shows MORE real crossings than T_sel = 0, the fidelity fix
    strengthens G1 rather than threatening it, and the audit's finding, while
    correct about the code, does not change the dossier's conclusion. That is a
    reportable outcome and it is not a failure of this job.
  - If crossings collapse to zero at both faithful settings, G1 as published
    does not survive and the go2 report's headline must be withdrawn.
  - If the two faithful settings disagree with each other (5 holds, 15 fails or
    the reverse), the behaviour is a function of a parameter we cannot measure
    on their hardware, and the honest verdict is "not decidable by this bench",
    stated as such rather than resolved by picking the friendlier value.
