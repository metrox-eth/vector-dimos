10.58 s pooled. 8 s is inside the per-map spread (hk_park 7.68 s is the
cheapest floor, hk_elevator 11.79 s the dearest), so the brief's figure is
consistent with what we measure, but it is not what we measure, and this file
reports what we measure. Neither value changes the sweep, which was fixed
before the measurement ran.

Under the pre-declared rule the median 10.58 s selects T_sel = 15 s for Stage B
(|10.58 - 15| = 4.42 against |10.58 - 5| = 5.58). Had the brief's 8 s been used
instead, the rule would have selected 5 s. Both faithful values are in Stage A,
so the reader can see the verdict at either one and is not asked to take the
choice on trust.

Measured at 4 m only. The recorded go2 bench's own per-run means put 12 m
decisions within about 1 s of 4 m ones on these four floors, so the anchor is
not range-sensitive here.
