=====================================================================
2026-08-29, offline, no robot, no git operation, nothing posted.

THE QUESTION
------------
The Go2 bench (../sim_2830_go2/rapport_go2.md) established that the cross-map
ping-pong is present under stock in the maintainer's own conditions, and that
the remedy (candidate M at GBPlanner's ratio, which on the Go2 body IS the whole
composite, section 6 of that report) removes some of it and not all of it. This
job asks one thing about what is LEFT:

    is there an "other half" of the ping-pong problem - a second fixable
    mechanism the remedy does not touch - or is the residual behaviour
    legitimate walking?

THE TAXONOMY, UNCHANGED
-----------------------
The classes, the radii and the code are ../sim_2830_fix/diagnose_swings.py, as
declared in ../sim_2830_fix/diagnostic_swings.md:

  N          not a crossing. The swing is defined on the GOAL sequence and the
             robot was not standing on the previous goal. Excluded before any
             share is computed, as in the fix job and as in the Go2 bench.
  A          a near frontier existed, was available, and lost the ranking.
  A-blocked  a near frontier existed but every one was suppressed by the harness
             failed-goal filter: the drive planner had already refused it.
  B          nothing near at the decision, but the cluster blinked out and comes
             back in the same place later.
  C          legitimate: nothing near, and nothing comes back.

  R_near     = 6.0 m of GEODESIC route length under the planner's own cost rule.
  SAME_SPOT  = 1.5 m.  REGION = 6.0 m straight line.

R_near is KEPT AT 6.0 m at both lidar ranges. At 4 m that is 1.5 lidar ranges,
the value the fix job justified; at 12 m it is 0.5. It is not re-tuned per
range, because a class share that moves with the radius is not a finding. The
sensitivity (R = 6 / 9 / 12 m) is printed instead.

"Fixable" = A + A-blocked + B. A-blocked is counted as fixable in the SHARE
because it is a mechanism failure and not geometry, and it is reported
separately everywhere so a reader who disagrees can subtract it: re-ranking
alone cannot reach an A-blocked decision.

WHAT RUNS
---------
Arms: stock and stock+M4.3 only. stock+M4.3 is the remedy; per rapport_go2.md
section 6 the reachability filter drops 0.1 % of candidates on the Go2 body and
stock+CMP was an identical run to stock+M4.3 on 53 of 54 paired starts, so
tracing M4.3 traces the remedy. That identity is re-checked run by run here
(cmp_m43_cmp.py) rather than assumed.

Ranges: 4 m and 12 m. Configs: shipped and scoring. Profile: go2, upstream 15 s
goal timeout, every dimOS constant untouched.

Maps: hk_office, hk_park, hk_elevator, hk_entrance. hk_allaround is out, as in
the Go2 bench: its scoring configuration never finished there and its 35.33 m
swing threshold is not answerable by a 4 m explorer.

Starts: the cells where the recorded Go2 bench produced a real crossing under
stock, stock+M4.3 or stock+CMP, unioned over the two configs so that no config
is decided by a start selected on the other one. Both arms are run on every
selected start, so every comparison is paired.

WHAT THE TRACE ADDS, AND WHAT IT MUST NOT CHANGE
------------------------------------------------
The trace is the Go2 bench's own --trace, plus two report-only fields:
  terms   the five weighted terms of the upstream score per candidate. Four are
          recomputed from the selector's own config and own read-only helpers;
          the obstacles term is DERIVED from the total, so the expensive square
          search is never run twice and the five sum to the returned total.
  policy  on a policy arm, the policy's own adjusted score per candidate, with
          the route length and direction deviation it used, recomputed from the
          policy's own methods immediately after its reorder() returned. It is
          needed because "the arm's own score" in class A means the policy's
          score on a policy arm, not the upstream one.

REQUIRED BEFORE SCALING: one traced run must reproduce its recorded counterpart
exactly, on a bare arm and on a policy arm. Then every traced run is compared to
its recorded counterpart, and the report states the number of disagreements
whatever it is.

HOW THE VERDICT IS READ, DECLARED IN ADVANCE
---------------------------------------------
1. Under stock, pooled over the maps and configs of a range: fixable share =
   (A + A-blocked + B) / real crossings. That is the answer to "is their
   explorer dumb, in their own conditions".
2. Under stock+M4.3: if A + A-blocked + B is 20 % or less of the residual real
   crossings, the fixable half is covered and no other half exists. If A alone
   is above 20 %, an other half exists and its decisions are dumped with their
   score ratios, distances and losing term.
3. Class-C crossings the remedy removed are counted cell by cell as
   max(0, C_stock - C_remedy), and the path and coverage of exactly those cells
   are printed, because a remedy that suppresses legitimate long walks pays for
   it in coverage.
4. Small integers. Every count here is a sum over a handful of paired starts.
   A difference of one or two events is not evidence on its own and is not
   reported as one.
