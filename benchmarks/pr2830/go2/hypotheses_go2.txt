0. WHY THIS BENCH EXISTS
================================================================================
Every bench in this workspace so far simulated OUR rover: 0.46 m wide, 0.15 m/s,
needing a 60 cm aisle. The maintainer's robot, and the robot that RECORDED every
floor here, is a Unitree Go2: 0.31 m wide, walking. Two consequences:

  - the "passable floor" the whole bench measures coverage against is a
    skeleton. Measured today at 4 m: the Go2 body opens +11 % to +99 % more
    floor on the eight admitted maps, +174 % on china_office.
  - goal_timeout x speed is how far a walk gets before the loop pulls the robot
    off it. At the UPSTREAM 15 s and our 0.15 m/s that is 2.2 m, which is why
    ../sim_2830_cand2/rapport_cand2.md section 8 found config `shipped` mostly
    measuring the timeout (real crossings 34 -> 12, median path -59.5 %). At the
    Go2's own recorded speed it is 9.0 m.

This bench re-judges the ping-pong question in THEIR conditions.

1. THE ROBOT PROFILE (go2_profile.py, "go2")
================================================================================
SPEED 0.60 m/s. DERIVED, not assumed. derive_speed.py reads the `odom` stream of
  dimOS' own recordings - the same stores the maps came from - through their own
  SqliteStore where the pose index columns are empty, and measures speed over
  sliding 0.5 s windows inside continuous segments, excluding standstill
  (window speed below 0.05 m/s). Pooled over the FIVE HK recordings: 18 733
  moving windows, MEDIAN 0.6029 m/s, p90 1.0387 m/s. The headline uses the
  pooled median, 0.60 m/s. Per-map medians run 0.428 (hk_office) to 0.945
  (hk_allaround); the alternative pooling, median of the per-map medians, would
  give 0.94 m/s. The pooled figure is the smaller of the two and is used for
  that reason: it is the choice least favourable to the claim that the shipped
  configuration becomes a usable instrument at Go2 speed.
TURN 0.63 rad/s. DERIVED by the same rule. A window where the body turns
  (yaw >= 0.10 rad/s) while NOT translating (speed < 0.05 m/s) is a turn IN
  PLACE, which is what explore_sim.TURN_RATE models; a walking curve is not.
  Pooled over the five HK recordings: 1 492 pivot windows, median 0.6287 rad/s,
  p90 1.2968. Turning spends goal-timeout budget, so this is not cosmetic: a
  180 deg turn costs 5.0 s instead of 6.3 s. The pivot sample is small on three
  of the five floors and hk_office carries 1 334 of the 1 492 windows; that is
  a caveat, the direction of the correction is not.
BODY 0.31 m wide, 0.70 m long. Unitree Go2 published standing size
  700 x 310 x 400 mm, identical across Air / Pro / Edu.
MARGINS, the harness' own rules kept proportional:
  ROBOT_WIDTH_M      0.50 -> 0.35    body + the same 4 cm planner margin
  BODY_HALF_WIDTH_M  0.23 -> 0.155   body / 2
  CONTROL_MARGIN_M   0.05 -> 0.05    unchanged, a controller margin
  LETHAL_CLEARANCE_M 0.30 -> 0.225   the same formula, width/2 + margin
  PIVOT_CLEARANCE_M  0.39 -> 0.383   the same rule, half the body diagonal
  (the Go2 is narrower but longer, so only the width-driven numbers move)
SCAN_EVERY_M 0.25 m, MEASURED AND DELIBERATELY KEPT. The objection this has to
  survive is that raising the speed while leaving a per-DISTANCE scan rate alone
  would be an optimistic discovery bias. It was measured, not asserted: from the
  recordings' own lidar frame timestamps against their own odom, the Go2 travels
  a median 0.0789 m (p90 0.1457) between two consecutive lidar frames, its
  stream running at 7.7 Hz. So 0.25 m of walk contains about 3.2 real
  revolutions at 0.60 m/s and about 12.7 at 0.15 m/s: the simulated
  one-per-0.25 m is the COARSER of the two at both speeds, information per metre
  is geometry-limited rather than sensor-limited, and keeping the step in
  distance isolates the robot profile from the sensor model. The residual, said
  plainly: the sim under-samples the real Go2 lidar by about 3x per metre, which
  is conservative about discovery, not optimistic.
  ARRIVE_M, BUMP_BACKUP_M and the harness stop conditions are unchanged.
NOT TOUCHED: every dimOS constant. The 0.25 m loop inflation, the 15 s goal
  timeout, the info-gain self-stop, the failed-goal radius are theirs and stay.
  Note the sign flip this causes and it is reported, not fixed: the costmap the
  scorers are handed is inflated 0.25 m while the Go2's lethal radius is
  0.225 m, so upstream's inflation is now CONSERVATIVE for this body where it
  was optimistic for ours (cand2 caveat 13).

4. ARMS

    stock            dimensionalOS/dimos @ 6fcc4e2, unmodified

Five arms. The two upstream selector files are read-only and stay byte-identical
(md5 e77c328643c959a49077115e8a341f2c and 1ccc0c69fe88a72e402565feca988d26).
Everything added is a harness-side wrapper installed on one selector INSTANCE.

THE COMPOSITE (fix_composite.py, spec `CMP`), three established pieces, no
fourth mechanism:

  (1) the signed direction term at GBPlanner's ratio 4.3. Sourced in
      ../sim_2830_fleet/confrontation_externe.md sections (iii) and (v).4:
      dimOS' own direction term is `max(0.0, dot_product)` at 5 % weight, so a
      U-turn costs exactly what a sideways move costs, nothing. GBPlanner
      weights direction 0.3 against 0.07 for path length. Won cand2's declared
      ratio sweep at 4.3.
  (2) the distance penalty, `exp(-0.07 * route_m)`, as in cand2's M0 control -
      the wrapper with the direction term switched off, which reached the same
      crossings and round trips as every ratio on bigoffice
      (rapport_cand2.md summary point 2). Pieces (1) and (2) are ONE formula in
      GBPlanner and one formula here, so `stock+CMP` and `stock+M4.3` differ
      ONLY by piece (3). fix_momentum.SignedMomentum is IMPORTED, not copied.
  (3) a reachability filter. A candidate whose goal cell the BODY cannot reach
      is dropped before ranking. One Dijkstra wave from the robot over the
      body-inflated costmap - explore_sim's own clearance_cost_map +
      clear_footprint + the planner's own cost rule, i.e. verbatim what
      Sim.plan_to builds to decide whether the goal is drivable. Precedent:
      explore_lite discards frontiers with no valid plan; nav2 refuses a goal
      that fails feasibility checking. Neither scorer under test models the body
      at all (cand2 caveat 13). SAFETY VALVE, declared and counted: if the
      filter would drop EVERY candidate, the list passes through unchanged.

  The policy reads exactly two things from the simulator through one explicit
  hook: the discovered occupancy grid and the robot pose - what a navigation
  costmap subscriber has on a real robot. No ground truth, no ceiling.

6. METRICS - the cand2 definitions, unchanged
================================================================================
REAL CROSSING (primary). Two consecutive published goals more than the map's
  swing threshold apart AND the robot actually travelled between them. The
  class-N filter removes the swings where the goal sequence jumped while the
  robot stood still (a goal timed out before the robot moved). Straight-line
  here, geodesic in ../sim_2830_fix/diagnose_swings.py; the two agreed on 63 of
  64 traced swings.
RAW SWING. The unfiltered count, printed beside the real one, never instead.
REAL ROUND TRIP. Three consecutive goals A -> B -> A' with A' within the
  threshold of A, all three legs real.
TOTAL PATH (m), COVERAGE (% of the Go2-passable ceiling), PATH TO 80 %.
DECIDE MS, and the policy's own counters.

RE-DERIVATION AND THE ZERO-DISAGREEMENT CHECK. Every crossing count in the
report is recomputed from the RAW published goal coordinates in the results
JSON by a second, independent pass (analyse), and compared against the counts
the bench recorded while running. The report states the number of runs where
the two disagree. The declaration made now: it must be ZERO, and if it is not,
the discrepancy is reported and the raw re-derivation is the number of record.

7. HYPOTHESES - verbatim, before any comparative run
================================================================================

G1: with the Go2 profile at 15 s shipped, the cross-map swing behavior the
    maintainer described is present under stock (real crossings > 0 on at least
    half the discriminating maps).

G2: stock+COMPOSITE reduces real crossings and round trips vs stock in each
    config, path within +5 %, coverage within -5 %, per map.

G3: pr2830+COMPOSITE same vs pr2830.

G4: COMPOSITE beats stock+M4.3 alone on at least half the discriminating maps
    without extra path beyond the budget.

READING RULES, fixed now so no verdict can be argued after the fact:
  - "discriminating maps" = the maps on which the crossing metric CAN fire,
    i.e. all HK maps except hk_allaround, whose 35.33 m threshold a 4 m explorer
    cannot reach. That is FOUR maps: hk_office, hk_park, hk_elevator,
    hk_entrance. "At least half" therefore means at least 2 of 4.
  - G1 is judged on config `shipped` only, since that is where the timeout
    lives and what "at 15 s shipped" names.
  - G2 and G3 are judged PER MAP AND PER CONFIG. A map fails if crossings do not
    fall, or round trips do not fall, or median total path rises by more than
    5 %, or median coverage falls by more than 5 percentage points. "Reduces"
    means strictly fewer where the baseline is above zero; a baseline of zero
    cannot be reduced and that map is reported as "no events to remove", not as
    a pass and not as a failure.
  - G4's "beats" = strictly fewer real crossings than stock+M4.3 on that map,
    with median total path no more than 5 % above stock+M4.3's.
  - Every per-map failure is printed. Nothing is pooled INSTEAD of the per-map
    table; pooled numbers may be printed BESIDE it.

WINNER RULE. No arm is called a fix on a pooled number. An arm is worth taking
to the maintainer only if it passes its hypothesis on at least half the
discriminating maps in BOTH configurations. Anything less is reported as what it
is: a result on some floors.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE WHOLE INVESTIGATION. If G1 fails - if even stock, on the
maintainer's robot profile, at the upstream timeout, on the maintainer's own
floors, produces no real crossings - then the behaviour this entire line of work
has been chasing is not reproducible in the conditions that matter, and that is
the headline finding of the job. It is to be reported first and not buried.
