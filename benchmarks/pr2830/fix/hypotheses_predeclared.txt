WHAT IS BEING TESTED
Two harness-side policy wrappers that re-rank the candidate list the upstream
selector produced. The upstream selector files are not modified.

  H, switch-margin hysteresis (precedent: explore_lite, Jiri Horner, ROS, in
     tree since 2017: distance from the robot is a first-class term of the
     frontier cost, with progress_timeout as the companion that prevents
     sticking). Implemented as: keep the best-scoring candidate within a
     6 m geodesic vicinity of the robot unless the best candidate anywhere
     beats it by a factor k on the arm's OWN score. Sweep k in {1.5, 2, 3}.

  B, finish-the-branch (precedent: segment / room-based exploration, Bormann
     et al., ipa_building_navigation / ipa_room_exploration: the robot finishes
     the segment it is in before the planner may move it to another). While any
     candidate lies within geodesic radius R of the robot, candidates outside R
     are not eligible. Sweep R in {6, 9} m. B is H with k = infinity.

Vicinity is measured as route length under the PLANNER's own cost rule
(explore_sim.plan: blocked at 100, unknown at 80), one wave from the robot per
decision, which is the shape explore_lite uses (one navfn potential field per
cycle, every frontier's cost read out of it).

HYPOTHESIS, per candidate, per base arm (stock and PR #2830 separately)
  Compared with the SAME arm without the wrapper, on the same paired starts:
   1. total cross-map swings must DROP.
   2. total round trips must NOT RISE.
   3. median total path must not be worse than +5 % relative.
   4. median final coverage must not be worse than -5 % relative.
  A candidate PASSES only if all four hold. Anything else is a failure and is
  reported as one.

SWEEP PROTOCOL
  Parameter sweeps: bigoffice only, 4 m lidar, the 6 middle starts, both
  configs, 24 runs per (candidate arm) sweep. Final winning configuration:
  both maps, 4 m, 6+6 middle starts, both configs, four arms
  (stock, stock+fix, #2830, #2830+fix). Regression: the winning fix at 12 m
  with the shipped bench's original starts, against ../sim_2830_bigoffice.

  No tuning past these values. If no candidate passes, that is the result.

WINNER SELECTION, declared before the sweep results were read
  Among the candidates that PASS all four conditions on the stock arm over the
  24 bigoffice runs: the largest absolute drop in total swings wins; ties broken
  by fewer total round trips, then by lower median total path. If none passes,
  no winner is declared and the report says so.
