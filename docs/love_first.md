# Love-first architecture

Love-first architecture is a design rule, not a slogan: build the emotional
substrate before scaling capability. The bond comes first, the skills come
after. Concretely, in our robots the joy of recognizing someone outweighs any
performance metric, and everything we add later inherits that base. Whoever
builds the tech imprints what it becomes.

The full hypothesis — the developmental sequence, the staged affect model,
the "ouch, I get it" principle, the ethics and the open questions — is in the
original paper (ShowRobotics × VitaNova, April 2026):

**[Love-first architecture — a developmental hypothesis for affective AI (PDF)](love_first.pdf)**

> We think the order in which you build intelligence matters.
> Love first. Pain second. And pain with a mute button.
