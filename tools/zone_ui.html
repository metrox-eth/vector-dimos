    How to use

    1. Draw: click "Draw a zone", then click one corner after another on the map.
    2. Close: double-click the last point, or the "Close" button.
    3. Name: a name, then "Save".
    4. Delete: click the zone in the list (or on the map), then "Delete".

    The rover re-reads the file by itself within half a minute: nothing to restart.
